# Privacy Policy

The Teddy Tales privacy policy is being prepared and will appear here in an upcoming update.

## What the app already stores

- Your e-mail address, if you signed up by e-mail.
- The date of birth you entered at sign-up — for the zodiac sign.
- Your pet's name and game progress: stats, coins, items, tasks.

Data is stored on the server so your pet comes back when you sign in on a new phone. The app does not sell data to third parties and shows no ads.

## Contact

To delete your account and data, or to ask a question, use the address shown on the app's store page.
